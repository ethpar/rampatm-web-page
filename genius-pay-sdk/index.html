For merchants

Accept stablecoins
with one SDK.

The GeniusPay™ Merchant SDK lets any point of sale, e-commerce checkout or ATM accept GENIUS Act Payment Stablecoins from any card or wallet on the network. Funds reach your account instantly — no chargebacks, no T+2 float, no new hardware.

  • Works on existing terminals — a software update for Genmega, PAI, Clover and Pax estates
  • Accept debit-card taps and wallet QR scans through the same API
  • Get paid in PSC or auto-convert to fiat — your choice, T+0 either way
  • Cryptographic finality — no disputes, no reversals, no chargeback fees

For wallets

Let any wallet spend
stablecoins in the real world.

The GeniusPay™ Wallet SDK plugs into any third-party wallet — custodial or self-custodial — and gives its users tap-to-pay at every terminal on the network and cash-out at GeniusPay™ ATMs. Keys stay where they are. We never touch funds.

  • NFC tap-to-pay and QR scan-to-pay at any GeniusPay™-enabled merchant
  • Cash-out to fiat at thousands of ATMs nationwide
  • Bring your own signer — passkeys, secure enclave, MPC or hardware
  • Only GENIUS Act Payment Stablecoins — compliance handled by the rails, not by you
Swift Kotlin React Native Flutter Web
@geniuspay/wallet

let genius = GeniusWallet(signer: PasskeySigner())

    amount:   48.50,
    asset:    .pscUSD
)

Faster underneath.

Card or wallet, the customer experience is what it is today. Only the network underneath changes.
