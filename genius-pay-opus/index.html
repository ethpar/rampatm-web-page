01. What we do

We connect your core banking stack to on-chain settlement — without touching custody, charters, or compliance.

Genius Pay routes stablecoin transactions through the core banking, card, and ATM infrastructure your institution already operates, and settles them instantly on-chain in regulated Payment Stablecoins.

Only stablecoins that qualify under the GENIUS Act of 2025 move across the network. Reserve backing, attestations, and auditability are properties of the rails themselves — not of a bolt-on report.

The result: a new settlement business for your balance sheet, delivered over systems you already trust.

04. How it works

Familiar on the outside. Faster underneath.

Customers keep their existing debit cards and accounts. Your systems keep their existing rails. Only the settlement layer changes — and it changes everything.

01

Authenticate

The customer presents a bank-issued debit card at any Genius Pay-enabled terminal or ATM. Identity checks are already complete — done by the issuing bank.

02

Route

The transaction travels to Genius Pay over existing payment rails. Where the network isn't available, it falls back to legacy processing without interruption.

03

Settle

Authorization returns in roughly 100 milliseconds. Settlement completes instantly in Payment Stablecoins on the Ethpar network — one immutable record for every party.
