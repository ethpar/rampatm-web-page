04 / How it works

Identical to legacy rails from the outside. Fundamentally better underneath.

Customer debit card Genius Pay On-chain PSC settlement

STEP — 01

Authenticate

The customer uses their existing bank-issued debit card at any Genius Pay-enabled terminal or ATM. KYC is already done — by the bank that issued the card.

STEP — 02

Route

The transaction routes to Genius Pay over existing payment rails. Where the network isn't available, it falls back to legacy processing seamlessly.

STEP — 03

Settle

Authorization returns in ~100ms. Settlement happens instantly in Payment Stablecoins on the Ethpar network — every party sees the same immutable record.

05 / The network

A new network that works with the ones you already have.

Genius Pay doesn't replace your core banking stack or card programs. It runs alongside them and settles what's worth settling on-chain.

Existing rails, upgraded

Genius Pay runs alongside existing debit, ACH, and card infrastructure on the same accounts and cards. Transactions preferentially use our rails and fall back to legacy networks when unavailable.

  • Dual-rail transactions on existing client channels
  • Automatic fallback to legacy processing
  • No changes to deposit accounts or card programs
  • Works with existing ATM and POS estates

Compliance-first by design

Built for regulated institutions from day one. Every stablecoin on the network qualifies under the GENIUS Act of 2025, with on-chain transparency your regulators can verify.

  • GENIUS Act Payment Stablecoins (PSCs) only
  • Full reserve backing with public attestations
  • Regulator-grade, on-chain audit trail
  • BSA/AML hooks at every transaction boundary
