How it works

Familiar on the outside.
Faster underneath.

Customers keep their cards. You keep your rails. Only the settlement layer changes.

1

Authenticate

The customer uses their bank-issued debit card at any Genius Pay-enabled terminal or ATM. KYC is already done — by the bank that issued the card.

// identity: verified by issuer
2

Route

The transaction travels to Genius Pay over existing payment rails. Where the network isn't available, it falls back to legacy processing seamlessly.

// fallback: automatic
3

Settle

Authorization returns in ~100ms. Settlement completes instantly in Payment Stablecoins on the Ethpar network — one immutable record for every party.

// finality: T+0
